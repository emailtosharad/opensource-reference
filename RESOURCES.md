## 🎵 Audio & Background Music Generation

### [Treblo](https://treblo.com/)
- **URL:** [https://treblo.com/](https://treblo.com/)
- **Category:** Free AI Background Music Generation
- **Description:** Web-based platform used for generating background music and ambient tracks without restrictive time limits.
- **Key Use Cases:**
  - Generating cinematic and ambient background tracks for video storytelling and voiceover narration (e.g. YouTube channels like Katha Sansar).
  - Creating royalty-free background audio beds for podcasts, videos, and presentations.
  - Generating continuous, longer audio textures without early cutoff limitations.
- **Notes:** Great resource for long-form narrative editing where standard 30-second AI preview limits are insufficient.
